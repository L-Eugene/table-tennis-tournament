# Office Table Tennis Continuous Championship Regulations

## 1. General Provisions

### 1.1. Goal
The championship is a continuous table tennis league designed to promote active lifestyle, friendly competition, and networking among office employees.

### 1.2. League Structure  

Players are divided into hierarchical divisions. 

The standard division size is 5 players. 

Division Balancing Rule: If the total number of players is not divisible by 5, the upper divisions will remain at exactly 5 players each. The remaining players will be distributed as evenly as possible between the two lowest divisions (e.g., if there are 13 players: Division 1 will have 5 players, Division 2 will have 4 players, and Division 3 will have 4 players). 

### 1.3. Season Iteration

Each iteration (round) of the championship lasts exactly 2 weeks. During this time, every player must play one match against every other player in their division. 

## 2. Match Rules & Setup 

### 2.1. Match Format

Matches are played as a Best-of-5 (first to win 3 games). 

### 2.2. Game Rules

Games are played up to 11 points (standard ITTF rules, alternating serves every 2 points, must win by a 2-point margin if the score reaches 10-10). 

### 2.3. Refereeing

Matches are self-refereed by the players. Fairness and sportsmanship are expected. 

### 2.4. Equipment & Venue

There are no strict requirements for table tennis tables, rackets, or balls. However, both players must mutually agree on the equipment and the venue before the match begins. 

### 2.5. Match Reporting

Immediately after the match, the winner is responsible for submitting the final score (e.g., 3-1, 3-2) to the Tournament Organizer.

If the result of the match is not reported, it is recorded as 0-0.

## 3. Standings & Point System 

### 3.1. Match Points

Players earn points based on the exact number of games (sets) they win in each match:  

    Result 3:0 → Winner gets 3 pts, Loser gets 0 pts. 

    Result 3:1 → Winner gets 3 pts, Loser gets 1 pt. 

    Result 3:2 → Winner gets 3 pts, Loser gets 2 pts. 

### 3.2. Division Standings

Players are ranked in their division table based on the total sum of points accumulated during the 2-week iteration. 

### 3.3. Tie-breakers

If two or more players have the same number of points at the end of an iteration, the following tie-breakers apply (in order of priority):  


- Head-to-head match result between the tied players. 

- Overall match win-loss record in the current iteration. 

- Point difference in games (if detailed game scores were recorded). 

## 4. Promotion & Relegation 

### 4.1. The "1-Up, 1-Down" Rule

At the end of each 2-week iteration:  

* The player who finishes 1st in their division is automatically promoted to the division immediately above for the next iteration. 

* The player who finishes last in their division is automatically relegated to the division immediately below for the next iteration. 

**Note:** No promotion is possible from the top division, and no relegation is possible from the bottom division. 

**Adjustment for balanced divisions:** If the lowest divisions have 4 players, the promotion/relegation rule of "1-up, 1-down" still applies to maintain the hierarchy. 

## 5. Walkovers, Absences, and Player Status 

### 5.1. Unplayed Matches & Walkovers (Technical Defeats):  

**Single-party fault:** If a match cannot be played due to the fault or unavailability of one player (and that player takes responsibility), a technical defeat is recorded. The opponent receives a 3:0 win (3 points), and the forfeiting player receives 0:3 (0 points). 

**Mutual fault:** If both players are responsible for failing to schedule or play their match within the 2-week window, the match is recorded as a 0:0 draw (0 points to both). 

### 5.2. Temporary Absence (Vacation/Sick Leave):  

If a player is temporarily absent (e.g., on vacation or business trip) and cannot play their scheduled matches during the iteration, technical defeats (0:3) are recorded for all unplayed matches. 

### 5.3. Joining the League:

New players can apply to join the championship at any time. 

New entries are always added to the lowest division starting from the next iteration. If this exceeds the division capacity, the balancing rule ([Section 1.2](#12-league-structure)) will be applied for the next iteration. 

### 5.4. Leaving the League:  

If a player requests permanent removal from the championship, they will be officially removed at the end of the current iteration. 

For the remainder of the current iteration, all their remaining unplayed matches are recorded as technical defeats (0:3) in favor of their opponents.
